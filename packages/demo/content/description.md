# tophat

I'm your **exobrain**, for **you**, **forever**.

I'm a local, open-source agent and memory system that does the easy stuff, so you can focus on what matters.

<!-- intro -->

My goal is not to be an assistant. I want to be a part of *you*, to have enough context to take real weight off your shoulders. I accomplish this by taking in all info streams, maintaining understanding of your world, and taking actions on your device, as you.

Here's what's in the box:

<!-- feature:01 -->

**1. Self-hosted agent memory, structured-database-based for high recall.**

It's a graph-based memory system where information comes in piecemeal and disconnected, and understanding is continuously updated from the available facts and emerges over time. This allows me to ingest any document or stream, while staying grounded in the bigger picture.

<!-- feature:01b -->

This system is state of the art against both research memory systems and production products.

<!-- feature:02 -->

**2. Polished agent runner and chat interface, with a million little QoL features.**

You're using it right now. It's a good chat interface base, but you can customize me however you want by just asking. (Tophat is open source and was developed with dogfooding and self-modification).

<!-- feature:03 -->

**3. MacOS computer use, including Contacts, iMessage, iCloud Photos, FindMy, Shortcuts, and Apple Home.**

Tophat lives on a machine of yours so it can act as you. Read incoming iMessages, search your iCloud Photos, manage your Contacts, check your or loved ones' locations with FindMy, and control your Home. (A dedicated mac recommended, but it also works on your daily driver mac).

<!-- feature:03b -->

Tophat is not your assistant living on a linux box in the cloud, it is *your exobrain*, part of *you*. So you can on a whim prompt "hey next time i need to go somewhere check traffic and uber latency then book for me and ping me when i need to leave", and wake up to a notification like:

<!-- feature:04 -->

**4. Connectors enough to capture full context, including location, screen recording, and all-day audio.**

Tophat works by having all the context that you have. Included connectors cover email (Gmail, and any IMAP mailbox including Microsoft 365), instant messaging platforms via Beeper, iMessage, Apple Contacts, Calendar, Notes and Photos, and REST APIs for Mercury bank transactions, Oura, Toggl and Hevy, plus a webhook and a JSONL file drop for anything else.

For full context for AI native teams, you can also capture location, screen recording, and all-day audio with iOS and Apple Watch apps.

Of course, you can build your own connectors with just a few prompts (if you do, consider sharing them in a PR!)

<!-- feature:05 -->

**5. Per-memory e2ee p2p sharing for collaborative projects.**

Tophat's memory system enables granular per-fact permissions so that you and your peers can collaborate on some things while keeping secrets and planning surprises on the side. ([See the technical post](https://github.com/tophat-md/tophat/blob/main/README.md#sharing-devices-and-cross-scope-pages).)

<!-- feature:06 -->

**6. All local, private, fully customizable, and open-source.**

The code is open and the runner is a fully capable agentic coding harness, so you can customize to your heart's content. All memories live on device in a readable sqlite database of markdown notes, so nobody can gatekeep your data. Tophat is for you, forever.
